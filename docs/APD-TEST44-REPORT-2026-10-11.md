## Cleric implementation
Players choose one through four saved quick-cast buttons in APD Settings, Hotbar layout. Spell one occupies the original blue button position above inventory; the remaining spells stack above it. Long-press the existing spell picker to assign a position; long-press a saved button to replace/remove it. Lowering the count preserves hidden preferences. Locked spells remain saved without granting access.
Existing Holy Tome targeting, costs, cooldowns and effects are reused. Assigning a spell does not cast it. Switching spells cancels the previous targeting selection. Existing classic/item category hotbar assignments, application ID, signing identity, saves and statistics are preserved.
