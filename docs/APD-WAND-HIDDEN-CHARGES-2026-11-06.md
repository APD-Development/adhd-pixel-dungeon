# Wand charge privacy — source only

Two real description leaks were reproduced. `/workspace/apd-wand-hidden-charges-before.log` shows Desperate Power changing Magic Missile's displayed damage from 4–12 to 2–8 according to whether its unidentified charge count was one or two. `/workspace/apd-wand-multi-charge-before.log` identifies Fireblast and Regrowth additionally exposing unidentified charge counts via their cost/effect descriptions.

The effective-level preview now includes both possible Desperate Power conditions whenever the current charge count is unknown. It does not consult that hidden count to select or narrow the interval. Known charges still produce the original exact level; actual buffedLvl/casting remains unchanged. The uncertainty note now describes conditional as well as random bonuses, rather than incorrectly attributing every interval to Wild Magic.

Fireblast and Regrowth withhold exact charge-dependent stat text while charges are unknown; the ordinary flavor description remains available. Once charges are known their original live stats return. One neutral privacy message was added and reviewed; the exact English census is 5,293, with all per-message clean/adult/format assertions retained.

Real effective levels remain 2 and 0 for the tested known conditions. Existing Wild Magic and complete authored-language tests pass.
