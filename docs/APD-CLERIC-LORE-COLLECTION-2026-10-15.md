# APD: How to Collect All 50 Cleric Lore Entries

Owner-only guide. Source inspected October 15, 2026. Includes spoilers.

## Verified contents

Exactly 50 unique authored entries: 18 anytime complaints, 16 sarcastic appreciation entries, 2 comparisons, 8 contextual entries, and 6 format jokes. Clean and Adult wording are variants of the same entry, not separate collectibles. The in-game counters total 49: the counter-discovery joke meta.0001 is deliberately excluded, but is included in this 50-entry guide.

## Why nothing appeared

The inspected build disables lore through ApdFeatures.GRUDGE_LORE_IN_THIS_RELEASE=false, and its private-build asset omits grudge. The first-run popup hook exists and runs when Start is pressed with Cleric selected, not when merely tapping the portrait. A correction enables explicitly flagged private builds while keeping normal-release behavior gated. This guide describes the existing collection rules; it does not claim the correction is already installed on your phone.

## Settings and collection

In a corrected private build, open APD Settings > About / Developer: The Grudge. Enable the locations you want, including Statistics screens if desired. Choose Rare for the more frequent existing option. Adult humor only changes wording. Do not use Forget what I've heard: that erases only the lore ledger and resets your collection, not gameplay statistics.

## Random appearances and real-time limits

Surprise messages are random, not guaranteed by meeting a condition. Default Rarer: one 15% eligibility roll per app session and at least 10 real days between surprises. Rare: 30% per session and at least 3 real days. Maximum one surprise per session. A 20-minute first-use grace period starts when the surprise system first encounters you. New sessions mean restarting the game process, not just returning to the menu. Unseen eligible entries are favored; no fixed collection order or completion date is guaranteed.

## Safe places

Main-menu/startup screens must settle and have no other popup. Statistics screens are an optional location. During a run, the hero must be alive, ready, idle, not resting or acting, with no visible hostile and no window; the floor cannot be locked or a boss floor. Quiet play requires 2.5 seconds. Consequently a boss entry cannot normally appear immediately on the boss floor: leave for a quiet non-boss floor after a newly detected defeat. After-run messages require the game's genuine death/victory results; abandoned runs do not substitute.

## The two exceptions

first.0001 appears once on starting a Cleric run and does not wait for surprise chance, cooldown or grace. It still respects Frequency Off and is remembered only after display. meta.0001 appears once when opening The Grudge counter screen. Both persist in the separate lore ledger across restarts/updates. No save editing, clock manipulation, repeated deaths, or gameplay changes are necessary to force collection.

## Long-term collection

There are 48 surprise entries plus the first-run entry and the counter-discovery entry. Even with perfect luck, Rare imposes at least 47 three-day gaps between 48 surprises (141 days), with the three long-delay story conditions also applying. This is an eligibility lower bound, not a realistic completion estimate: randomness and the contextual requirements make it longer. The current engine can consume a boss/run-result opportunity without choosing that entry; repeat the genuine qualifying event later. This guide does not silently change those existing rarity rules.
