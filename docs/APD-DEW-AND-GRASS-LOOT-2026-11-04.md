# Dew and grass-loot pickup scope — November4,2026

Owner clarified that automatic collection includes dew already lying on a tile the hero walks over, seeds dropped by grass, and Petrified Seed runestones dropped by grass. Dew plus seed (or dew plus stone) on one tile must both collect. No drop-rate or capacity increase was requested.

## Implementation

- Preserved existing saved `apd_auto_grass_dew` as the shared master toggle and existing visible-enemy override. No new preference switches/default migrations. Regular Options UI now says Dew and grass loot settings; its two-checkbox native dialog scrolls when needed.
- A completed voluntary step offers ordinary existing ground dew. HighGrass notifies the same queue for its existing seed/stone roll and dew roll. The original RNG calls, probabilities, item generation and order remain intact. Independent offers on the same heap extend the queue and deduplicate actual item identities; a second drop does not replace the first.
- At the next hero action, after movement/enemy turns/FOV, collect at most one queued stack using its ordinary `doPickUp`/`pickupDelay`. Movement plus seed plus dew therefore spends normal movement plus two pickup actions. Every subsequent pickup rechecks visible enemies, preferences, hero/action/position, level and heap. A newly visible enemy cancels remaining automation unless the existing override allows it.
- Dew requires a present non-full Waterskin and never auto-heals or auto-drinks. Full/missing Waterskin does not prevent collecting a seed/stone. Seeds/stones use normal bag capacity and stack merging. Failed/full pickups leave loot on the ground, spend no pickup turn and are not retried automatically. Chests, hidden/sale heaps and unrelated loot are excluded. Existing non-grass seed/stone piles are not vacuumed by the dew-on-step path.
- Automation remains transient, releases its level/heap/action references after completion/cancellation, and is not serialized as a pending player action. Forced movement does not initiate collection. Saves, item state, statistics/scoring and existing normal pickup effects remain governed by their normal systems.
